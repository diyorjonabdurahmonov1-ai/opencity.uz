Add AI photo features: category suggestion and resolution proof check

New /api/analyze-photo.js Vercel serverless function calls OpenAI
gpt-4o-mini vision (OPENAI_API_KEY kept server-side, never exposed to
the browser). Two uses:

- ReportWizard: after the first photo is added, suggests a category
  based on the image and lets the citizen apply it with one click.
- OrganizationPortal: each resolution proof photo is checked for
  signs of being AI-generated or otherwise not a genuine on-site
  photo; suspicious ones get a warning badge and banner (non-blocking,
  trust remains with the organization).

Both calls fail silently (return null) if the API/key is unavailable,
so the app degrades gracefully without it configured.
